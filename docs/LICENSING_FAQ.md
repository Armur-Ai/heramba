**Can I run it to protect my own internal systems?**
Yes — internal use is a Permitted Purpose under FSL. (Internal-use permission is
**not** a blanket answer for offering multi-tenant managed protection to others —
see below.) ⚖️

**Can I use it to protect my company's own public-facing application?**
Yes, protecting your own application is permitted. ⚖️

**Can I offer installation, configuration, or support services?**
Qualifying professional services are generally permitted under FSL; offering the
software *itself* as a competing commercial product/service is not. The line
between "services around the software" and a "competing managed service" is
fact-specific. ⚖️

**Can I run it as a managed security service for my customers?**
This is the core **Competing Use** question and is **not** automatically allowed.
Do not assume every MSP/proxy deployment is exempt, and do not assume every one
is prohibited. ⚖️ Required before relying on an answer.

**Can I fork it?**
Yes. Forks remain under FSL for the current version and convert to Apache-2.0 on
each version's second anniversary.

**What about older versions?**
Each version becomes Apache-2.0 on its conversion date (see
`legal/PUBLIC_RELEASES.json`). After conversion that version may be used,
including hosted, under Apache terms.

**Is this "open source"?**
It is **source-available** under FSL, not OSI-approved open source. Converted
(Apache-2.0) versions are open source.
