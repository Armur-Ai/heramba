The Corporation agrees:

1. **Verified entity & authorized signer.** Provide exact legal entity name,

2. **Grant.** On the same terms as the ICLA §3–§4 (copyright + patent license to

   individual must sign the ICLA separately.
4. **Contributor roster (Schedule A).** List covered contributor roles/people;
   describe how roster changes are notified prospectively.
5. **Current PRs / historical schedule.** Identify PRs covered on signing;
   earlier Contributions require the historical schedule. No backdating.
6. **Representations.** The Corporation has authority to grant the above and will
   identify third-party/upstream material and its license.
7. **Incorporated ICLA text/hash.** Record the exact ICLA version text + SHA-256
   incorporated by reference, effective date.
8. **Successors / persistence of public grants.** As in the ICLA.

Capture via the approved corporate e-signature process (not this file); a green
