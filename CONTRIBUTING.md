## Ownership and licensing of contributions

- **You keep copyright in your contributions.** Through the applicable signed CLA
  (`../CLA.md` for individuals, `../CORPORATE_CLA.md` for employer-owned work),
  you grant Armur Technologies Inc. a broad license to use, modify, sublicense,
  and distribute your contribution under current/future source-available,
  open-source, **and proprietary** terms — including FSL's future Apache grant.
- This means **accepted contributions may be incorporated into Armur's commercial
  products.** We will not promise otherwise. Direct incorporation happens only
  when the rights ledger supports it.

## Before you contribute

- A signed CLA is required before external contributions are merged (process:
  `docs/legal/CLA_PROCESS.md`). The process is **pending activation**.
- **Disclose**: any third-party/upstream code and its license, any AI-assisted
  material, and whether your employer may own the work.
- **Do not** include confidential or customer material, secrets, or real attack
  data.
- Any security/active-defense testing must be run in an **isolated lab against
  systems you own** — never against third parties. See
  `docs/ACTIVE_DEFENSE_BOUNDARIES.md`.

## Upstream code

The right to submit upstream code (e.g., AGPL-licensed) is **not** the right to
offer it under proprietary/FSL terms. Flag anything you did not author.

## PRs
Follow `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`. All material authors of a PR
(including co-authors) must be covered before merge.
